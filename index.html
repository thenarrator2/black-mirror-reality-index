How this is scored

The numbers are judgment, not measurement. Each episode gets a 0–100 reading of how much of its core mechanism now exists in the world, weighted toward deployment rather than demos: a lab result moves a line a little, a product people actually buy moves it a lot, a government procurement moves it more. Trajectories are anchored to dated real-world events, listed under each episode, and interpolated between them. Two people doing this honestly would disagree by ten or fifteen points on most rows.

Scores deliberately track the mechanism, not the plot. "Mazey Day" is graded on celebrity surveillance and not on werewolves. "Demon 79" is in here as a control: it has no technology in it at all, so its line should stay flat, and it does.
